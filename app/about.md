Non-Profit Public Good Will Academic Resource for the Editorial pages from e-newspapers published by **The Hindu** and **Indian Express**.

# Disclaimer

**Purpose.** This site is a non-commercial, academic-purpose resource built to help students preparing for competitive examinations in India — UPSC, state PSCs, banking, and similar — access newspaper editorials, a core and time-sensitive part of current-affairs preparation. It is maintained for public education and is not operated for profit.

**Ownership.** All newspaper content reproduced or linked here — text, layout, images, and PDFs — remains the intellectual property of its respective publisher (**The Hindu**, **Indian Express**, and any other source referenced on this site). No ownership, authorship, or affiliation is claimed over any such content. Nothing here is sold, sublicensed, or monetized in any form.

**Legal basis.** Use of copyrighted material on this site is intended to fall within the *fair dealing* exception under **Section 52 of the Copyright Act, 1957** (India), which permits reproduction for purposes including private study, research, and educational use, provided such use does not affect the commercial value of the original work. Content is sourced exclusively from material already publicly accessible online — no paywall circumvention, unauthorized access, or scraping of subscriber-only content is performed. This site operates as an intermediary aggregating and linking publicly available third-party content, consistent with **Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000**, and will act expeditiously on any valid takedown request.

**Retention.** Content is retained for a rolling 7-day window and then automatically and permanently removed. This is an access aid for timely exam preparation, not a permanent archive, mirror, or substitute for subscribing to the original publication.

# Takedown Policy

**Takedown requests.** If you are a copyright holder, an authorized representative, or otherwise believe content on this site infringes your rights, contact **vaultscapes@gmail.com** with:
1. Identification of the copyrighted work and the specific URL(s) on this site;
2. A statement of your relationship to the rights holder (owner / authorized agent);
3. Your contact information.

Valid requests will be actioned within **48 hours**, including removal of the specific content and, where applicable, adjustment of the extraction workflow to prevent recurrence. This process is intended to satisfy takedown obligations under both Indian law and, given this site's hosting infrastructure, the U.S. **Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)** where applicable.
